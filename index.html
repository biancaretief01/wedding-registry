Having you there to celebrate with us is what we’re most looking forward to. If you’d also like to give us a gift, we’ve gathered a few things we’d love for our home.

Some are specific favourites; others are just a nudge in the right direction—a colour, a style or an idea. We haven’t chosen every detail, because we’d love to see what you would choose for us, too.
